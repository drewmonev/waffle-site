Terms

Last updated 23 August 2026

This is the agreement between you and the person who makes Waffle. It is short, and it is written the way the privacy policy is written: in plain language, because an agreement nobody can read is not an agreement.

The short version

  • Your photos stay yours. Posting one does not hand it over. Waffle gets only the narrow permission it needs to store it and show it to the friends you chose.
  • A photo is permanent after 24 hours. You can delete it before then. After that the only way to remove it is to delete your account. This is the point of the app, not a missing feature.
  • You need to be 13 or older. Waffle is not built for children and accounts are not knowingly made for them.
  • Nothing charges you today. Payments are not connected yet. If that changes, it will be an Apple subscription and you will see the price before anything happens.
  • It is a beta. It is on TestFlight, run by one person. Things can break, and you should not treat it as the only copy of a photo you care about.

Who this agreement is with

Waffle is made by Batter, a one-person project based in San Jose, California. On this page “Waffle” means the app and this site, and “we” means that one person.

Questions, complaints, or anything that needs a human: hello@feedwaffle.com. A real person reads that address.

Using Waffle means you accept what is on this page. If you do not, the app is not for you, and deleting your account in Settings ends the arrangement.

Who can use Waffle

  • You must be 13 or older. If you are under 18, you should have a parent or guardian read this with you.
  • One account per person. Your handle and name are how friends recognise you, so do not pretend to be someone else.
  • Your account is yours to look after. Anything done from it is treated as done by you. If you think someone else is in it, email the address above.

Your photos stay yours

You own what you post. Nothing here transfers ownership, and nothing here lets your photos be sold, advertised against, or used to train anything.

What you do give is a narrow, practical permission: to store your photos, resize them, and show them to the specific people you chose, for as long as they are on Waffle. That is the permission required to run the app, and it does not reach any further. It ends when the photo is deleted.

You also confirm you have the right to post what you post: that it is yours, and that anyone recognisable in it is content to be there.

The 24-hour rule

This is the most important thing on the page, so it gets its own section.

A pocket can be deleted for 24 hours. After that it is permanent. Not hidden, not archived. Permanent. This is enforced in the database rather than by hiding a button, which means no support request can undo it and neither can we.

Deleting your whole account is the only way to remove a photo that has set. That works, and it removes the image files rather than only the records pointing at them.

Worth saying plainly: take the 24 hours seriously. Waffle is built so that showing up is permanent, and that promise only means something if it holds in the moments you would rather it did not.

Waffles you share

A waffle can have more than one person in it. Inside a shared waffle, everyone’s photos belong to whoever posted them. You cannot save, download, or export someone else’s, and that is enforced rather than merely discouraged.

If you delete your account, your photos go with it, including the ones you added to a waffle you share. The waffle itself carries on for everyone else, with your squares gone from it.

What you agree not to do

  • Post anything illegal, or any sexual content involving minors. There is no second chance on this one.
  • Post someone else’s private or intimate images, or anything a recognisable person has not agreed to.
  • Harass, threaten, or impersonate anyone.
  • Try to reach data that is not yours, get around the friends-only rules, or probe the database. If you find a way through, email it to us instead. That is a favour, and it will be treated as one.
  • Automate, scrape, or run the app through anything other than the app.

Blocking, hiding, and reporting

You can block someone, hide a single photo, or report something. Blocking is mutual and immediate: neither of you sees the other’s photos afterwards, including inside a waffle you share, and including photos already posted.

Reports are read by a person. We can remove content or close an account that breaks the rules above, and where it is possible to say why, we will.

Waffle Plus

Free accounts get one photo a day across all your waffles, and one waffle you started. Plus lifts that to one photo a day into each of them. Joining a waffle a friend started is free on both, permanently.

Nothing charges you right now. Payments are not connected to the app yet, and Plus is switched on by hand for the people who have it. If billing is added later it will run through Apple’s subscriptions: you will see the price and confirm it before anything is charged, it renews until you cancel it in your Apple account settings, and refunds are handled by Apple rather than by us.

This is a beta

Waffle is on TestFlight and is made by one person in their own time. That means features can change or disappear, the app can break, and in the worst case data can be lost. Do not let Waffle hold the only copy of a photograph that matters to you.

Ending it

You: Settings → Delete account, any time, no reason needed.

Us: we can suspend or close an account that breaks these terms or puts other people at risk. If the account is closed for something serious the content goes with it.

Waffle is provided as it is. We do not promise it will always be available, always be free of bugs, or never lose data, and we do not make any guarantee beyond what the law requires us to make.

To the fullest extent the law allows, we are not liable for indirect or consequential losses, or for lost photos or data. Nothing here removes rights you have under the law that cannot be signed away, including any consumer rights in the state where you live.
