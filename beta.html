Come and test Waffle

One habit, one photo a day, seen only by the friends you add. No streaks, no counters, no strangers. It is not on the App Store yet, so getting in takes four steps and about five minutes.

Getting in

  1. Send me the email address your Apple ID uses Not necessarily the one you email from. It has to be the Apple ID on the iPhone you want Waffle on, or the invite will not open.
  2. Wait for an email from TestFlight It comes from Apple, not from me, and the subject line has Waffle in it. Check your junk folder if it has not turned up within a few hours.
  3. Install TestFlight Apple's free app for trying apps before release. Get it here, or search the App Store for TestFlight. You only ever need it to install updates.
  4. Open the invite and tap Install Waffle lands on your home screen like any other app. Make an account with your email, confirm it from the message we send, and you are in.

Before you start

  • You need an iPhone on iOS 16.4 or newer. There is no Android version and no iPad layout yet.
  • Photos come from the camera, in the moment. There is no way to add one from your camera roll. That is the whole idea rather than a missing feature.
  • Nobody sees anything unless you add them. No explore page, no public profile, no follower list. If you have no friends on it yet, your feed is supposed to be empty.
  • Bring a friend. Waffle only really works once someone you know is on it too, so it is worth joining in twos and threes.
It is a beta, made by one person. Things will break. If something looks wrong, tell me: Settings has a Send feedback row, or email support@feedwaffle.com. A real person reads it, and a bug you mention is one I can actually fix.

What it does not do

  • Nothing happens when you miss a day. No alert, no broken badge, no guilt. The number of days simply is not there any more.
  • Nothing costs money. Payments are not connected, so nothing in the app can charge you.
  • No notifications come from a server. Any reminder you get is made on your own phone.
