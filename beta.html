Before you start

Four things worth knowing.

What to expect

  • An iPhone on iOS 16.4 or newer. There is no Android version and no iPad layout yet.
  • Photos come from the camera, in the moment. There is no way to add one from your camera roll. That is the whole idea rather than a missing feature.
  • Nobody sees anything unless you add them. No explore page, no public profile, no follower list. An empty feed is correct until you add someone.
  • You need at least one other person. A feed with nobody in it is correct and also pointless, which is why invites go out to groups rather than to whoever asked first.

What it does not do

  • Nothing happens when you miss a day. No alert, no broken badge, no guilt. The number of days simply is not there any more.
  • Nothing costs money. Payments are not connected, so nothing in the app can charge you.
  • No notification comes from a server. Any reminder you get is made on your own phone.
  • It will break sometimes. It is a beta made by one person. Settings has a Send feedback row, or email support@feedwaffle.com. A real person reads it.
